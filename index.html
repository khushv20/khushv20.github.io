Selected work

Strategy, Fundraising, and Government Relations

  • Fundraising

    Raised nearly Rs 5 crore over four years for SAFAR from Azim Premji Foundation, Foundation for Ecological Security, International Budget Partnership, Samagata Foundation, and individual philanthropists.

  • Organisation leadership

    Lead SAFAR as Director. 10 full-time staff embedded in national and state campaigns, with a Rs 2 crore annual grant portfolio.

  • India's first gig worker law

    Led the campaign, stakeholder consultations, and drafting support behind the Rajasthan Platform Based Gig Workers Act, 2023. Supported similar legislation in Karnataka. Telangana, Jharkhand, and Bihar have since started parallel efforts.

  • Government advisory

    Advised 5 central ministries and 9 state governments on social protection, digital public infrastructure, public finance, commons governance, and social accountability.

  • National social audit policy

    Shaped the Ministry of Social Justice's Social Audit policy, now covering all ministry programmes with dedicated funding, by piloting audit protocols, training modules, and an MIS.

  • Social enterprise strategy

    Repositioned the product-market narrative of Gurujal, a rural water access social enterprise, for its funding rounds and annual fundraising showcase.

  • Convening

    Convened 10 national Commons Accountability Dialogues with 200+ researchers, technologists, and practitioners.

Programme Design and Management

  • District grievance redress, Adilabad

    Designed and piloted a decentralised grievance redress system across 7 departments, 20 grievance categories, and 16 mandals in Telangana. Handled about 13,000 grievances and 80 public hearings. Achieved a 48% positive disposal rate on actionable grievances, with 68% resolved or escalated.

  • Right to Education, Gujarat

    Ran Indus Action's state-wide implementation, securing school access for 1,00,000+ children from marginalised communities.

  • Training at scale

    Designed and delivered 20+ training programmes for 2,500+ practitioners and students. The social audit curriculum co-developed with civil society is now used by the Governments of Bihar and Karnataka.

  • Social audits

    Designed and facilitated social audits across sectors and states, with MIS platforms built for audit teams.

Measurement, Evaluation, Accountability, and Learning

  • Results frameworks

    Wrote results frameworks for programmes funded by Azim Premji Foundation, Samagata Foundation, and International Budget Partnership. Designed Gurujal's organisational MEL framework, used in its funder reports and funding rounds.

    Designed the public audit of relief payments. Built an IVR system reaching 2,35,000+ listed beneficiaries and a multilingual portal for citizens to verify claims and file grievances. Found 47% of listed beneficiaries received no payment.

Research and Policy Analysis

  • Urban commons frameworks, Pune and Shillong

    For International Budget Partnership, produced social accountability frameworks for urban commons governance through fieldwork with civil society, urban local bodies, and affected communities. Civil society and local authorities in both cities adopted them.

  • Housing equity, Houston

    Led an interdisciplinary team assessing 90 low-rent housing complexes. Recommendations informed city council-approved reforms, including an improved grievance system, for 16,000+ residents.

  • Governance diagnostics, Haryana

    Ran implementation diagnostics across 12 government departments for the Chief Minister's Office. Contributed research inputs to the Haryana Community Participation and Public Services Social Audit Act, 2018.
